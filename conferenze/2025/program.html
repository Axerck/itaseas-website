5th ITASEAS PROGRAMME

University of Turin 4-6 June 2025

Welcome to ITASEAS 2025, the 5th conference of ITASEAS, held at the University of Turin, Campus Einaudi 4–6 June 2025.

Download PDF Program View Sessions Table

The detailed program includes three plenary speeches and 21 sessions distributed across three days. In parallel, the TO-ASEAN Culture Days will present a rich program of roundtables and book launches.
